✦ Cultural Tips

  • Always remove shoes before entering homes and temples
  • The "Wai" greeting (palms together, slight bow) shows respect
  • Never touch someone's head — it's considered sacred
  • Point with your whole hand, not a single finger
  • Dress modestly when visiting temples (cover shoulders & knees)
  • Royal family references must always be respectful — this is law
  • Use "Krap" (male) or "Ka" (female) for politeness at the end of sentences
  • Thai food is shared; take small portions from central dishes
  • Avoid using your left hand to pass money or food
  • Never point your feet at people or religious items
